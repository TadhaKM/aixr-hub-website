Meet the Trinity AI XR Lab Team

Get to know the people who bring the Lab to life. Our team combines academic expertise, creative problem solving, and a shared commitment to helping learners build the human skills needed for the future of work.

Our Lab Directors

Our Lab Directors provide academic leadership, strategic direction, and a clear vision for how AI and XR can support responsible, human-centred learning. They guide the Lab’s research agenda, teaching innovation, and partnerships across Trinity and beyond.

Professor Na Fu

Director, Trinity AI XR Innovation Lab
Chair of Responsible Leadership and Fellow, Trinity Business School

Na Fu is a leading scholar in responsible leadership, digital transformation, and people management. She has published widely, secured major national and EU funding, and works globally with industry and academic partners. Her work focuses on building responsible, human-centred organisations.

LinkedIn

Dr Laura Berry

Manager, Trinity AI XR Innovation Lab
Assistant Professor of Digital Marketing and AI

Laura Berry’s research focuses on how new technologies create value for firms and consumers. Drawing on over a decade of industry experience she bridges academic research with practical application. At Trinity Business School, she is academic director for the MSc in Digital Marketing Strategy and combines rigorous research with hands-on learning approaches to help students build future-focused skills for the evolving digital landscape.
