Trinity AI XR Hub

Immersive Intelligence. Positive Impact.

A facility within Trinity Business School where students, businesses and researchers build the skills that stay distinctly human. Immersive simulations and AI-supported coaching strengthen communication, leadership and judgement in an environment that is safe, realistic and deeply engaging.

How the Hub Works

Grounded in Trinity’s commitment to innovation with purpose, the Hub combines human-centred design, ethical technology adoption and immersive, hands-on learning.

For Students

We help students strengthen the human skills that underpin good judgement and leadership through immersive, AI-supported learning.

For Businesses

We support organisations with tailored training programmes that use AI and XR technologies to build essential human skills.

For Research
