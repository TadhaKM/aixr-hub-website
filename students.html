Build the Human Skills That Shape Your Future

The Trinity AI XR Hub supports students across Trinity Business School with immersive, practice-based learning experiences that help you develop the communication, leadership, and interpersonal skills needed for the future of work. Through VR-supported sessions, scenario-based learning, and guided practice, you can strengthen the abilities that make a real difference in professional settings.

Book a Session

Curriculum-Integrated Teaching

Our XR-supported sessions are embedded into core modules across selected TBS programmes, bringing classroom concepts to life through immersive scenarios that mirror real business situations.

Students can expect to:

  • Practise communication and leadership in realistic case-based environments
  • Build soft skills such as empathy, negotiation, teamwork, and decision making
  • Develop presentation skills, personal pitch delivery, and professional presence
  • Apply course concepts in hands-on, interactive settings

These sessions are currently being designed with academic leads across multiple programmes.

Skill Development Sessions

We work closely with Programme Directors, the TBS Careers Team, and Student Support to run dedicated sessions throughout the year — for MSc, PhD, MBA, and undergraduate students.

CV Building

Guided activities and VR practice help you refine your CV, structure your experience, and communicate your strengths clearly.

Interview Practice

VR-supported mock interviews give you the chance to rehearse responses, improve delivery, and build confidence in high-pressure scenarios.

Communication & Soft Skills

Improve clarity, storytelling, presence, and rapport building — with scenario-based conversations ranging from client meetings to elevator pitches.

Teamwork & Leadership

Interactive, immersive tasks support collaborative problem solving, decision making, and leading under pressure.

Drop-In Sessions

Students who complete onboarding can book independent practice time in the Hub.

  • Practise interviews, presentations, and pitches
  • Rehearse difficult or unfamiliar conversations
  • Explore VR tools for personal skill development
  • Build confidence at your own pace
