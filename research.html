Exploring the Human Side of Technology

The Trinity AI XR Hub provides a collaborative space for researchers interested in how AI, VR, and emerging technologies shape human behaviour, learning, leadership, and professional performance. Our goal is to support high-quality research that advances understanding of human capability in digitally enabled environments.

Enquire About Research

Primary Research

The Hub offers facilities, immersive tools, and support for researchers investigating communication, decision making, leadership, teamwork, well-being, and human–technology interaction. VR-supported scenarios allow for controlled, repeatable environments — ideal for behavioural observation and experimental design.

Researchers can explore:

  • Communication and interpersonal dynamics
  • Leadership behaviour in high-pressure settings
  • Teamwork, collaboration, and problem solving
  • Ethical judgment and decision making
  • Human responses to digital and immersive environments

We welcome project proposals from across business, psychology, education, and interdisciplinary areas.

Research Opportunities

Faculty Research

Integrate immersive methods into your research agenda. Whether conducting experiments, observational studies, or pilot projects, the Hub provides a dedicated space to test ideas, collect data, and explore new research methodologies linked to digital transformation and human capability.

Student Research

Undergraduate, postgraduate, and PhD students can access the Hub for thesis projects, capstones, or independent research — with guidance on immersive tools, access to equipment and space, support for study setup and data collection, and opportunities to join ongoing initiatives.

Business Research

The Hub collaborates with industry partners exploring how employees learn, adapt, or perform in digital settings. Organisations can pilot ideas, run small studies, or contribute to research on skills development, digital learning, and leadership.

Interdisciplinary Collaboration

We support teams working across business, psychology, education, and beyond — examining the human side of technology and the future of work.

Why Conduct Research With Us

  • Access to immersive VR environments
  • Support from trained staff and research-informed processes
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration opportunities
  • Alignment with Trinity Business School’s Transform Business for Good strategy
  • A focus on responsible, ethical, human-centred research
