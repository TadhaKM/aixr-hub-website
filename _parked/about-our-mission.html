Our Mission

Our mission is to act as a test bed for digital and AI-enhanced education innovation across Trinity. The Hub is already attracting interest from schools and disciplines across the university that are exploring immersive learning and AI integration in their own teaching.

Our ambition is to grow into a centre of excellence nationally and internationally. We aim to shape not only the future of business education but also the broader role of technology in society. By fostering collaboration, encouraging responsible innovation, and supporting evidence-based practice, the Hub contributes to a more thoughtful and human-centred approach to digital transformation.
