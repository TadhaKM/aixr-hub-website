A Hub to Shape Human Capability in an AI-Powered World

The Trinity AI XR Hub is a pioneering facility within Trinity Business School at Trinity College Dublin. It serves as a hub for immersive learning, human skills development, and responsible innovation — helping learners build the essential human capabilities needed for the future of work.

We focus on the complementarity between technology and human capability. While AI can automate many tasks, the skills that matter most — communication, emotional intelligence, leadership, and ethical judgment — remain distinctly human. The Hub strengthens these skills through immersive simulations, realistic practice environments, and AI-supported coaching.

We support students preparing for leadership roles, businesses seeking innovative training solutions, and researchers exploring how technology can enhance human potential. The Hub brings together educators, industry partners, and interdisciplinary teams who are working to amplify the human side of digital transformation.

Our Story

The Trinity AI XR Hub was created to explore a simple but vital idea. Technology is advancing rapidly, but the ability to lead, communicate, collaborate, and make sound judgments remains the foundation of professional success. The Hub was established to develop these capabilities using tools that mirror the realities of modern work.

Our approach combines academic research, immersive learning design, and responsible AI practice. By integrating AI-enhanced simulations and XR-supported environments into teaching, the Hub provides opportunities for learners to practise difficult conversations, navigate complex scenarios, and reflect on their decisions in a space that is safe, realistic, and deeply engaging.

Our Mission

Our mission is to act as a test bed for digital and AI-enhanced education innovation across Trinity. The Hub is already attracting interest from schools and disciplines across the university that are exploring immersive learning and AI integration in their own teaching.

Our ambition is to grow into a centre of excellence nationally and internationally. We aim to shape not only the future of business education but also the broader role of technology in society. By fostering collaboration, encouraging responsible innovation, and supporting evidence-based practice, the Hub contributes to a more thoughtful and human-centred approach to digital transformation.

Our Goal

Our goal is to create a learning environment where technology strengthens what makes people effective at work. We believe that future leaders will need both technical fluency and strong human capability. The Hub exists to support this development by helping learners think critically, act responsibly, and build confidence through practice.

We are committed to preparing students, organisations, and researchers for a world where success depends on the ability to connect human judgment with technological insight.

What We Offer

A dynamic environment for exploring how VR and AI-supported tools can strengthen human capability in education, professional development, and research — focused on communication, leadership, teamwork, confidence building, and practical skill development.

Immersive Skills Training

VR-supported sessions that build communication, leadership, teamwork, and decision-making skills — from job interviews to boardroom debates, Dragon’s Den-style pitches, and high-stakes client meetings.

CV & Career Preparation

Through VR practice and guided activities, learners refine their CVs, strengthen their personal pitch, and build the skills needed to present themselves effectively during recruitment.

Interview & Communication Practice

Rehearse interviews, presentations, and public speaking in a focused, realistic environment — improving clarity, structure, body language, and confidence.

Curriculum-Integrated Experiences

We collaborate with faculty to embed VR and immersive learning into academic modules, giving students practical opportunities to apply course concepts.

Training for Organisations

Short, targeted training experiences for businesses focused on communication, collaborative problem solving, leadership, and ethical decision making.

Research Support

Access to the Hub’s space, VR equipment, and tools for faculty, student, and industry research on the human side of technology and the future of work.

Open Hub Access

Students who have completed onboarding can attend drop-in sessions to practise interviews, presentations, and communication skills independently, at their own pace.
